Archetype

Refiner

The ball does not lie. It goes where it is loved — and no one loves it more than this.

Animal: Crocodile

Still, exacting, decisive the instant the chance appears. Reads its environment, waits for the opening, strikes with speed.

How It Moves

The Refiner's eyes light up the moment the ball arrives. They'll take one touch more than anyone expects, not from hesitation but from a search for the perfect solution. Patient until precisely the right instant, their connection to the ball is instinctive, expressive, and entirely their own.

Why This Formation — 1-4-3-3

Room to breathe, triangles everywhere, freedom to combine or go alone.

At Its Best

Discernment, knowing when their skill should open space for themselves, and when it should serve the collective rhythm.
