Archetype

Avatar

Mastery is not the end of the journey. It is the moment you realize the journey never ends.

Animal

No single animal, because it carries all seven at once, the Elephant's grounding, the Crocodile's precision, the Ram's fire, the Stag's balance, the Dolphin's intelligence, the Owl's perception, and the Eagle's freedom.

How It Moves

The Avatar isn't chosen, it emerges. It's what appears when a player has fully known their own energy and learned to move between all seven others as the moment requires. There's no fixed position and no fixed formation, because where others are shaped by the game, the Avatar shapes it.

At Its Best

Perfect adaptability in service of whatever is needed, whenever it's needed, in harmony with the flow of the game.
